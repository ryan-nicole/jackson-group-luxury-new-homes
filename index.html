FAQ

Questions buyers ask first.

When should I contact Jackson Group?

Ideally before your first builder or model-home tour so we can help you start with a clear strategy and protect your options.

How much can my new home rebate be?

The amount depends on the home price, builder compensation, your representation agreement, lender requirements, and the final structure of the transaction.

Can Jackson Group help me sell my current home too?

Yes. The website will include a seller pathway so buyers who need to sell first can coordinate both sides of the move.

Can I search by city and builder?

Yes. Community pages will show builders in that city, and builder pages will show the cities where each builder is active.
