Seller FAQ

Questions that come up before the move.

Can Jackson Group help me sell and buy new construction?

Yes. The seller experience is designed specifically to coordinate the sale of your current home with your next-home search.

Do I need to sell before I start looking at new homes?

Not necessarily. The right sequence depends on your timeline, finances, builder schedule, and risk tolerance. We can help you think through the order before you make commitments.

Can I still receive a new home rebate if I am also selling?

Your home sale does not automatically determine rebate eligibility. Any rebate depends on the compensation and terms of the new-home purchase transaction.

Will the website give me an instant home value?

No. We are intentionally using a consultation-based approach rather than presenting an automated number that may not reflect your home’s actual condition, location, updates, or market context.
