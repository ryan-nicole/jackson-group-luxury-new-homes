The Process

Seven simple steps.

Every buyer's transaction is different, but the Jackson Group experience follows a clear framework so you always know what comes next.

01

Start with a city, builder, community or broader home-search goal. Use the Jackson Group Search Homes page to explore the market without feeling overwhelmed.

Register Before You Tour

Register Before You Tour →
04

Get Your Builder Form

Your completed registration generates a Jackson Group builder form you can print or download. The future backend will also email the PDF automatically.

05

Tour & Compare

Visit communities, compare builders, floor plans, available homes, incentives and timing while keeping your broader goals in view.

06

Build the Best Offer & Savings Strategy

Jackson Group helps you evaluate pricing, builder incentives, available options and potential rebate opportunities before moving forward.

07

Contract → Closing → Rebate

Once you're under contract, we help guide the transaction through milestones, closing and the rebate process when applicable.

Questions Buyers Ask

Quick answers before you begin.

Do I need to register before touring?

Jackson Group recommends connecting before your first model-home visit so representation, builder strategy and potential rebate opportunities can be discussed from the beginning.

Can I search homes before registering?

Yes. Browse cities, builders and community guides freely, then register before you decide to tour a builder.

What if I don't know which builder I want?

Start with Search Homes or the Community Guide. You can explore by city first and compare builders later.

Can Jackson Group help if I need to sell my current home?

Yes. The seller pathway is designed to coordinate your current-home sale with your next purchase.
