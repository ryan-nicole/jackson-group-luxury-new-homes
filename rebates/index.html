Register Before You Tour

Your New Home. Claim Your Rebate.

Buying a brand-new home? Timing is everything. Register with Jackson Group / Mersal Realty before contacting any builder or signing a contract to secure your mandatory eligibility for a closing credit rebate.

Once you execute a contract or visit a model home unaccompanied, it is too late to qualify for a rebate under builder commission policies. Take the first step before your first visit.

Register to Claim Your Rebate → Rebate eligibility depends entirely on builder cooperating commission policies, representation agreements, and loan terms. Your estimated savings will be formalized and confirmed as we prepare your formal rebate paperwork.
