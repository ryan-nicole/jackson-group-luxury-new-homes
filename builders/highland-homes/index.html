About Highland Homes

A Texas builder focused on design, livability, and customer care.

Highland Homes builds across Austin, Dallas–Fort Worth, Houston, and San Antonio-area markets. Its public materials emphasize architectural beauty, customer service, thoughtful design, energy efficiency, and choosing communities that support the homeowner’s lifestyle.

From a buyer’s perspective, that means Highland can appeal to shoppers who want a broad mix of one- and two-story plans, design-focused homes, established community options, and quick move-in inventory depending on the location.

Highland Homes FAQ

Questions buyers ask first.

Where does Highland Homes build in Texas?

Highland currently lists communities across the Austin, Dallas–Fort Worth, Houston, and San Antonio regions. Jackson Group will organize those by city as the platform expands.

Does Highland Homes offer quick move-in homes?

Yes. Highland publishes quick move-in homes and community inventory on its official website, with availability changing over time.

Does Highland Homes offer a warranty?

Highland currently publishes a six-year structural, two-year mechanical, and one-year functional warranty program. Buyers should review the actual warranty documents provided with their purchase.

Can I receive a rebate when buying a Highland home?

A rebate may be available depending on the compensation and transaction structure. Register before touring so Jackson Group can review the details with you.

When should I contact Jackson Group?

Before your first model-home visit whenever possible so representation, builder strategy, and rebate planning can be discussed from the beginning.
