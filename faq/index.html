About Jackson Group

How we help.

What does Jackson Group specialize in?

Jackson Group helps buyers explore homes across Texas with a strong emphasis on new construction, builders, communities, rebate planning and the overall purchase process.

Do you only help with new construction?

No. New construction is a major focus, but Jackson Group can also help buyers search broadly for homes in the cities featured on the platform.

How much real estate experience does Jackson Group have?

Jackson Group is backed by more than 30 years of real estate experience.

Can Jackson Group help me sell my current home too?

Yes. If your next purchase depends on selling your current home, the seller pathway is designed to help coordinate both sides of your move.

New Home Rebates

Understanding the opportunity.

What is a new home rebate?

A new home rebate is a potential buyer benefit that may be available depending on the compensation structure, buyer representation agreement, builder policies, lender requirements, transaction terms and applicable law.

Is every buyer guaranteed a rebate?

No. Rebate eligibility and amount vary by transaction. The Rebate Center is designed as a planning tool, not a guarantee.

When should I ask about a rebate?

Before touring a builder whenever possible. That gives Jackson Group the best opportunity to explain your options before your first model-home visit.

Can I estimate my rebate online?

Yes. The Jackson Group Rebate Center includes an estimator you can use as a planning tool before registration and touring.

When would a rebate be received?

Timing depends on the final transaction and how the rebate is structured. Jackson Group can explain the specific timing once the terms of your purchase are known.

Builder Tours

Before you walk into the model home.

Why should I contact Jackson Group before touring a builder?

Connecting first helps Jackson Group understand your goals, discuss rebate opportunities, compare builders and communities, and help protect your options from the beginning.

What if I already visited a builder?

You may still have options. Contact Jackson Group and explain which builder and community you visited, whether you completed paperwork and whether you identified an agent during the visit.

Do I need Jackson Group with me at the model home?

The exact process can vary by builder and transaction. The important first step is connecting with Jackson Group before your visit so the representation strategy is clear.

Can Jackson Group help compare different builders?

Yes. The Builder Guide and Community Guide are designed to help buyers compare builders, cities and communities before deciding where to tour.

Registration

Start before you tour.

What does Register Before You Tour mean?

It means completing your Jackson Group buyer registration before visiting the builder so your search, builder interest and buyer information are organized from the beginning.

What happens after I register?

The current system generates a builder-form experience that can be printed or downloaded. The future backend will also email the completed form and sync the registration to Wise Agent CRM.

Can I choose more than one builder or area?

Yes. The system is being designed around buyers who may be comparing multiple builders, cities and communities before deciding where to purchase.

Do I need to know exactly which home I want before registering?

No. You can register while you're still comparing cities, builders and communities.

Search Homes

Finding the right place.

How can I search homes on the website?

Use Search Homes to browse by city, builder or goal. You can also start with the Community Guide or Builder Guide if you prefer to explore that way.

Which cities are currently included?

The current platform includes Georgetown, Round Rock, Hutto, Liberty Hill, Leander, Taylor, Cedar Park and Weir, with more cities easy to add later.

Will available homes update automatically?

Not yet. The current public pages are static. The future inventory system is planned to pull approved builder inventory into the website automatically.

Can I search resale homes too?

Yes. The city pages are designed to support broader home-search intent in addition to Jackson Group's new-construction focus.

Selling Your Current Home

Buying and selling together.

Can Jackson Group help if I need to sell before buying?

Yes. Jackson Group can help coordinate your listing strategy, builder timeline, purchase timing and next move.

Do I have to sell before I start looking?

Not necessarily. The right sequence depends on your goals, finances, timing and the builder schedule.

Can I still receive a rebate if I'm also selling my home?

Potentially. Rebate eligibility is tied to the purchase transaction and its terms, not simply to whether you are also selling.
