FAQ

New-home rebate questions, answered simply.

How much can I receive back?

The amount depends on purchase price, compensation structure, your agreement, lender rules, builder policy, and the final transaction.

Should I register before visiting a builder?

Ideally, yes. Builder representation and registration policies can affect whether buyer-agent representation and a rebate are available.

Can I keep builder incentives?

Builder incentives vary and should be verified for the specific community, home, and transaction.

Is the calculator a guarantee?

No. It is an illustrative planning tool until actual transaction terms are verified.
