About Georgetown

A Texas city with room for your next chapter.

Georgetown combines a recognizable historic core with an expanding collection of new-home communities. Buyers can move from downtown streets and local dining to trails, riverfront recreation, shopping, and newer master-planned neighborhoods without leaving the city.

For new-construction buyers, that variety is what makes Georgetown especially useful to explore: the right fit may come down to builder, neighborhood design, amenities, school district, lot style, or how you want your everyday routine to feel.

Jackson Group’s role is to help you compare those choices and begin with a clear strategy before your first model-home tour.

Yes. Georgetown has multiple active new-home communities and builders, and availability changes as communities add phases and inventory.
